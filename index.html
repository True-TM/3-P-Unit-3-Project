Millionare's shortbread

What is Millionare's Shortbread?

Millionare's Shortbread is an originally Scottish dessert. It started as just Scottish Shortbread before caramel was brought into the mix in around the 15th-16th century. The first official recipe for Millionare's Shortbread, a shortbread cookie type dessert with a layer of Shortbread, a layer of caramel, and a layer of chocolate, was published in Scotland in 1972. The recipe was revised and republished in 1981, where it became an official dessert that was fully available to the public to make for themselves as the form it's in today, with a layer of chocolate ganache on top.
